1. What Are LSI Keywords & How Does Google Use Them?

LSI keywords are conceptually related terms and phrases that search engines use to deeply understand the context of a webpage. For instance, if your article discusses "Tesla," search engine bots analyze the surrounding text for semantic entities such as "Elon Musk," "electric vehicles," "Gigafactory," "lithium-ion batteries," and "Supercharger network" to confirm that the page relates to the automotive company rather than the inventor Nikola Tesla.

  • Basic Keyword Recommendations: Fundamental industry vocabulary and primary topical phrases that Google expects to appear in standard content covering the subject.
  • Extended NLP Semantic Terms: Advanced contextual entities extracted via Google's Natural Language Processing API that establish topical completeness and authority.
